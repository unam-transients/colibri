# 2025-11-14 Infrastructure Notes

## Harmonics

Edgar and I looked at the harmonics:

- One of our outages of the American UPS coincided with an event in which the suppressor system was not working correctly.
- We see the European UPS use its batteries most nights just after 08:00 UTC = 00:00 local.

Edgar, Joel, Luis, and I went to the telescope. We turned off the AC units and fan and then both UPSes while monitoring the harmonics. Turning off the AC units worsened the harmonics by perhaps 1%, possibly because resistive loads help. Turning off the UPSes improved the harmonics by perhaps 1%, possibly because inductive loads do not help. The net impact seems to be minor.

Edgar changed the American UPS to a mode that suppresses harmonics.

## UPS Discharge and Recharge Times

We were able to measure the charge and discharge times with the telescope not operational and the autonomay for 80% of charge.

|UPS|Discharge 10%|Recharge 10%|Autonomy 80%|
|---|---|---|---|
|American|21 m|180 m|168 m|
|European|15 m|166 m|120 m|

We see that we have about 2 hours of autonomy for the telescope and about 2.5 for the instrument (including the compressors).
